Truck Tracker: Tracking Your Tracking Business

Our target client for this hackathon is our friend's father's trucking business, or trucking businesses in general.

The main purpose is to enrich the client's tracking experience with a 3D-rendered map of their deployed trucks, see their statuses, speed, and location on the map.

Features, not in order, and not limited to:
    - Render the map in website using 3JS so more intuitive
        - New way of showing fleet
    - Slap on a GPS on the truck
        - Return information to 3JS
    - Track truck movements on the map
    - View truck details
    - View truck's "quest"- which client it goes to
        - Track driver's and AI-automated reports under each quest
    - Estimate route from Point A-B, or N
    - ETA
    - If truck wanders too far from the optimized route, send alert
    - GPS is normally horizontal, if becomes vertical- which is very unlikely
        - means truck turned over, send alert notification
        - tapping on alert notification does:
            - directs the user to the truck's location
            - option to call driver
            - automated emergency call regarding vehicular crash
                - if no response from driver after a set amount of time
    - Estimate gas price
        - based on truck's current location
        - based on truck's estimated gas consumption
        - based on truck's estimated distance to travel
    - Estimate tolls
        - based on truck's current location
        - based on truck's estimated distance to travel
    - Estimate expenses
        - based on truck's current location
        - based on truck's estimated distance to travel
        - based on truck's estimated gas consumption
    - If for some reason, the final cost is diverts too far from the estimation
        - may raise grounds for staff's mishandling of resources
    - AI implementation
        - If  detected that a truck deviated far from its optimized route     
            - Trigger AI agent to search why they may have deviated 
                - E.g. Road closure in Ortigas every weekends
                    - If the usual route goes through the road and truck deviates far enough
                    - AI will add a note under the deviation report that the road is closed during weekends
        - Truck deviation query for drivers
            - Upon deviation alert, query the driver
            - They may send a voice message, auto-parsed by AI
            - They may send an image, auto-captioned by AI
            - The image will be pinned beside the truck's 3D model in the map for easier intent resolution
    - Weather API fetching + dynamic update on 3JS site
        - Raining on truck X general location -> rain effect on truck effect radius
